About the Bootcamp

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar is organizing a five-day bootcamp titled “DART: Drone Applications, Research & Technology” October 13 TO October 17, 2025 at Punjabi University, Patiala, Punjab, funded by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. The bootcamp is designed to provide participants with a balanced understanding of drone technology, covering core concepts such as drone components, assembly, flight operations, safety, and security aspects, along with insights into diverse applications in fields like agriculture, logistics, surveillance, and infrastructure monitoring. Through expert-led sessions and hands-on activities, the program aims to enhance practical skills and prepare participants for opportunities in the rapidly growing drone ecosystem.

About MeitY Drone Project

The project 'Capacity Building for Human Resource Development in Unmanned Aircraft Systems/Drone & related technology’ titled as 'SwaYaan' is a National Initiative by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to develop and strengthen the UAS/Drone ecosystem in India. The vision is in line with the Government of India's initiative to make India a global Drone hub by the year 2030.
