About the Bootcamp

In a notable effort to enhance proficiency in unmanned aircraft systems, a thorough 5-day BootCamp is presently taking place at Dr. B R Ambedkar National Institute of Technology, Jalandhar, spanning from December 18th to 22nd, 2023. The event is an integral part of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology; initiative, Capacity Building for Human Resources in Unmanned Aircraft System (Drone Related Technology).An exceptional feature of the BootCamp was the incorporation of sessions led by industry experts, and participants also had hands-on sessions with the latest hardware. This combination of academic excellence and industry insights elevated the learning experience, providing participants with a comprehensive understanding of drone applications and security measures. With 46 active enthusiasts participating, the BootCamp fostered an environment for collaborative learning and skill development. The knowledge shared during the event is expected to significantly enhance participants' expertise in unmanned aircraft systems and related technologies.

About MeitY Drone Project

The project 'Capacity Building for Human Resource Development in Unmanned Aircraft Systems/Drone & related technology’ titled as 'SwaYaan' is a National Initiative by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to develop and strengthen the UAS/Drone ecosystem in India. The vision is in line with the Government of India's initiative to make India a global Drone hub by the year 2030.
