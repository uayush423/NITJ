About the Bootcamp

The "Drone Assembly, Security, and Application" Bootcamp, held from June 3rd to June 7th, 2024, has successfully concluded, bringing together 70 participants eager to explore the cutting-edge world of drone technology. This immersive program provided an in-depth exploration of drone assembly, focusing on the technical aspects of building and customizing drones, along with essential security measures to ensure safe and efficient drone operations. The bootcamp also covered the wide-ranging applications of drones across various industries, from agriculture and logistics to surveillance and environmental monitoring. In addition to comprehensive theoretical learning, participants had the opportunity to engage in hands-on drone technology exercises, allowing them to apply their knowledge in real-world scenarios. The event was guided by expert instructors, ensuring that every participant walked away with valuable skills and insights to advance in the rapidly growing field of drone technology. It was a rewarding experience that provided both practical expertise and a deep understanding of the security and applications of drones in today's world.

About MeitY Drone Project

The project 'Capacity Building for Human Resource Development in Unmanned Aircraft Systems/Drone & related technology’ titled as 'SwaYaan' is a National Initiative by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to develop and strengthen the UAS/Drone ecosystem in India. The vision is in line with the Government of India's initiative to make India a global Drone hub by the year 2030.
