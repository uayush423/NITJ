About the Bootcamp

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar is organizing a five-day bootcamp titled “Smart Drones: Autonomy, Sensing & Applications” from 20th to 24th November, 2025, at Sri Sri University, Cuttack, Odhisa,funded by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. The bootcamp is designed to provide participants with a balanced understanding of drone technology, covering core concepts such as drone components, assembly, flight operations, safety, and security aspects, along with insights into diverse applications in fields like agriculture, logistics, surveillance, and infrastructure monitoring. Delivered by experienced industry experts and academic professionals, the bootcamp aims to build technical competence, foster innovation, and prepare participants for emerging opportunities in the rapidly advancing field of unmanned aerial systems. Overall, the program serves as a highly impactful and skill-oriented learning experience for all attendees.

About MeitY Drone Project

The project 'Capacity Building for Human Resource Development in Unmanned Aircraft Systems/Drone & related technology’ titled as 'SwaYaan' is a National Initiative by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to develop and strengthen the UAS/Drone ecosystem in India. The vision is in line with the Government of India's initiative to make India a global Drone hub by the year 2030.
