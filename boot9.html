Bootcamp 9.0

About the Bootcamp

The "Drone Dynamics: Drone Technology, Trends, and Applications" Bootcamp, held at Guru Nanak Dev University in Amritsar, Punjab, from January 20th to January 24th, 2025, has successfully concluded with 63 participants. This comprehensive bootcamp delved into the latest advancements in drone technology, offering participants a detailed understanding of drone design, assembly, and operational dynamics. The program also focused on the emerging trends in drone applications, exploring how UAVs are transforming industries such as agriculture, logistics, surveillance, and more. Participants not only gained valuable theoretical knowledge but also engaged in hands-on activities that provided real-world experience with drone technology. Led by expert instructors, the bootcamp proved to be an enriching experience, equipping participants with the skills and insights required to stay ahead in the rapidly evolving drone industry. It was a highly successful and informative event for all involved.

About MeitY Drone Project

The project 'Capacity Building for Human Resource Development in Unmanned Aircraft Systems/Drone & related technology’ titled as 'SwaYaan' is a National Initiative by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to develop and strengthen the UAS/Drone ecosystem in India. The vision is in line with the Government of India's initiative to make India a global Drone hub by the year 2030.
