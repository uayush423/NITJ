About the Bootcamp

The “DroneSphere: Technology & Applications” Bootcamp was successfully conducted from November 03 TO November 07, 2025, at the Dr B R Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar. This intensive program engaged 33 participants and provided them with comprehensive training on drone technology and its various applications. The bootcamp covered a range of essential topics, including the fundamentals of drone technology, basic piloting techniques, safety and legal aspects, and the assembly of drone components. Participants gained hands-on experience in flight controllers, sensors, and programming drones, with practical sessions in drone assembly and troubleshooting. Additionally, advanced topics such as the integration of advanced sensors and payloads, drones in emergency response and public safety, as well as their applications in commercial and industrial sectors, were explored. The bootcamp also highlighted future trends in drone technology and discussed the emerging business and entrepreneurship opportunities within the drone industry. This initiative aimed to equip participants with the skills and knowledge necessary to excel in the growing field of drone technology.

About MeitY Drone Project

The project 'Capacity Building for Human Resource Development in Unmanned Aircraft Systems/Drone & related technology’ titled as 'SwaYaan' is a National Initiative by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to develop and strengthen the UAS/Drone ecosystem in India. The vision is in line with the Government of India's initiative to make India a global Drone hub by the year 2030.
