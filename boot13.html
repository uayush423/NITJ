About the Bootcamp

Dr. B R Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar is set to organize a five-day bootcamp on "DFTA: Drone Flight Technology and Applications" from August 04 TO August 08, 2025, at ,Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala, Punjab. Funded by MeitY, This bootcamp provided an in-depth exploration of drone technology, covering everything from the basics of drone assembly and flight operations to their wide-ranging applications across different sectors. Participants gained a strong understanding of the technical aspects of building drones, the security measures required for safe operations, and the potential uses of drones in industries like agriculture, logistics, surveillance, and more. The bootcamp also offered hands-on experience, allowing participants to apply their theoretical knowledge in practical scenarios. Led by industry professionals, the event was an enriching experience that equipped participants with the skills and insights necessary to thrive in the ever-evolving field of drone technology. The event proved to be a valuable learning opportunity for all those involved.

About MeitY Drone Project

The project 'Capacity Building for Human Resource Development in Unmanned Aircraft Systems/Drone & related technology’ titled as 'SwaYaan' is a National Initiative by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to develop and strengthen the UAS/Drone ecosystem in India. The vision is in line with the Government of India's initiative to make India a global Drone hub by the year 2030.
